Couch, Mattress & Bulky Furniture Removal

Junkernauts Junk Removal picks up couches, mattresses, tables, desks, dressers, recliners, and other bulky furniture across Southeast Michigan. We handle single-item pickups as well as furniture left after a move, rental turnover, or room clear-out. Our crew carries the approved items out, loads the truck, and handles hauling and disposal.

Do I Need to Put Furniture at the Curb?

No. Tell us whether your furniture is inside the home, upstairs, in a basement, in a garage, or already outside. Include photos of large pieces and mention narrow stairs, tight doorways, elevator access, and parking restrictions so we can plan the removal. You can leave the lifting to the crew.

The pool table photo on this page shows one of our removal jobs. For a pool table or another unusually heavy piece, send photos and access details with your furniture pickup estimate request so we can confirm the scope before scheduling.

How Much Does Furniture Removal Cost?

The quote depends on the truck space your items use, the handling required, and disposal needs. A single piece may fall under our minimum pickup; several rooms of furniture may need a larger load. See our minimum pickup and load-size pricing guide, then send an item list and photos for an estimate. We confirm the exact price before loading begins.

Mattress and Box Spring Pickup

Include the mattress size, number of pieces, and whether a box spring or bed frame also needs to go. Tell us if the items are upstairs, in a basement, or already outside, and describe their condition. We will confirm the handling and pickup plan with your estimate.

We provide furniture pickup in Ypsilanti, Ann Arbor, Westland, and other Southeast Michigan service areas. The curbside photos below show a mattress and boxed household items removed together; inside-home pickup is also available.

Donation, Recycling & Larger Cleanouts

We separate reusable furniture and recyclable material when practical. Donation depends on the condition of the items and available options; furniture that cannot be reused is hauled for disposal. Let us know if there are pieces you want to keep so they stay out of the removal load.

For an entire home or inherited property, see estate and property cleanouts. If the job is mainly desks, conference tables, or business furniture, see commercial junk removal. You can also call or text 734-480-8190 to discuss your pickup.
