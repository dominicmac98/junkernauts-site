Furniture Pickup Questions

Do I need to put furniture at the curb?

No. We pick up inside homes, apartments, basements, and garages too. Tell us about access when requesting your estimate.

Can you take a mattress and box spring?

Yes. Include the mattress size, number of pieces, condition, and whether a bed frame also needs to go.

Will my furniture be donated or recycled?

We separate reusable furniture and recyclable material when practical. Donation depends on condition and available options; other items go for disposal.

What if I have more than furniture?

We also handle house and estate cleanouts and commercial junk removal. Let us know which items should stay.
