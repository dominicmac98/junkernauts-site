Basement Cleanout Questions

Do I need to bring items upstairs?

No. Our crew handles carrying items out. Include especially heavy pieces and any access restrictions in your estimate request.

Can you remove basement renovation debris?

Yes. Tell us about drywall, insulation, flooring, and lumber separately so we can plan handling and disposal. See construction debris removal.

Can you clear other rooms too?

Yes. List the rooms and items in your request. Our house and estate cleanouts cover a broader scope.

Are items donated or recycled?

We separate reusable and recyclable materials when practical. Donation depends on condition and available options; remaining items go for disposal.
