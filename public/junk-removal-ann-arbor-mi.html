Junk Removal in Ann Arbor, MI by Junkernauts

Ann Arbor junk removal often comes with an access challenge, not just a pile of items. Older homes can have narrow basement stairs, campus-area rentals may have limited parking, and move-out projects can involve a tight schedule. We plan around the details you give us, then handle the lifting, carrying, loading, and hauling so you are not left making repeated dump runs or wrestling bulky items to the curb.

We help with one-off furniture and appliance pickups as well as larger garage, basement, rental, estate, and renovation cleanouts. A couch from a Kerrytown apartment, a room full of storage from the Old West Side, or debris from a basement project each needs a slightly different plan. Tell us where the items are located, whether there are stairs or an elevator, and anything especially heavy or hard to reach.

Pricing is based on the space your items take in the truck, the material being removed, and the access at the property. We can provide a photo-based price range when you send pictures, then confirm the exact price onsite before any work begins. Small pickups are covered by our minimum, while larger Ann Arbor projects are quoted by the load.

Our Ann Arbor work is not limited to household clutter. We also handle rental turnovers, landlord pickups, office furniture, construction debris, and cleanup after remodeling. When items are usable, we look for donation or recycling options where practical; the remaining materials are hauled for responsible disposal.
