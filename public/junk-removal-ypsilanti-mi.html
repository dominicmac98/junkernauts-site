Junk Removal in Ypsilanti, MI by Junkernauts

Ypsilanti jobs tend to be practical, real-life cleanouts: a rental needs to be cleared between tenants, a garage has become long-term storage, an older basement is packed with furniture, or a move leaves behind the bulky things that regular trash cannot take. Junkernauts handles the lifting, loading, hauling, and disposal so you can move the project forward without renting a truck or making multiple dump trips.

We work with homeowners, renters, landlords, property managers, and small businesses across Ypsilanti. From homes near Depot Town and Normal Park to apartments, rental turnovers, and garages around the city, every pickup starts with the same useful information: what needs to go, where it is located, and whether there are stairs, tight turns, or limited parking.

Common Ypsilanti requests include couches, mattresses, dressers, old appliances, shelving, bagged clutter, garage overflow, basement storage, move-out leftovers, and renovation debris. We can take a single bulky item, a compact pile, or a larger cleanout. If the material is unusual, heavy, or difficult to access, send a photo so we can make a realistic plan before arrival.

We price by the truck space your items use, combined with the materials and access needed for the job. A fast estimate from photos gives you a useful price range; an onsite quote gives you the exact number before we begin. Nothing is loaded until the price is clear and you approve it.

For Ypsilanti rental and property cleanouts, timing matters. We can coordinate an appointment window, clear the requested items, and leave the area swept up when practical. Usable items are separated for donation or recycling when possible, while the rest is routed to responsible disposal.
