Before Your Pickup

Is the appliance still connected?

Tell us if power, water, or gas is still connected before scheduling. We will confirm the preparation needed; disconnection is not assumed to be included.

What about built-in or oversized appliances?

Send photos of the appliance and surrounding space. Include dimensions and any stairs or narrow doorways so we can confirm the removal plan.

A straightforward pickup

Clear pricing before we load

Appliance Removal Questions

Do appliances need to be outside?

No. We collect from kitchens, laundry rooms, basements, and garages too. Access details help us plan the crew and equipment.

What happens to the old appliances?

We consider reuse and recycling where practical, with remaining items taken for responsible disposal. Mention damage or unusual conditions in your estimate request.

Can you quote a larger cleanout in person?

Yes. Schedule a free onsite quote if the full scope is difficult to show in photos.
