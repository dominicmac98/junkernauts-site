Furniture and mattress removal in Westland

Junkernauts Junk Removal helps with a single bulky item or several pieces left after a move or room clear-out. We pick up couches, recliners, mattresses, box springs, dressers, tables, and other unwanted furniture. See our furniture removal service and curbside pickup photos for an example of a completed job.

For a mattress pickup, include its size and whether a box spring or bed frame also needs removal. For a couch or sectional, send a photo of the whole piece and tell us about stairs, narrow doorways, or tight turns. That helps us estimate both the truck space and the work needed to carry it out.

Inside pickup or a staged pile: you choose

You do not have to move heavy furniture to the curb. We can collect approved items from inside your home, a basement, a garage, or an outdoor staging area. Let us know where the items are and where the truck can park. This is a privately scheduled junk removal service, not a municipal trash collection route.

How much does a Westland junk pickup cost?

Pricing depends on truck space, material type, and access at the property. A small pickup may fall under our minimum, while several rooms or a larger pile may need a bigger load. Check the pickup minimum and load-size guide and send photos for a price range. We confirm the exact price onsite before loading, and you decide whether to proceed.

More than furniture to remove?

Include everything in one request. We also offer appliance removal, garage cleanouts, basement cleanouts, and estate and property cleanouts. For removed flooring, drywall, or other remodeling leftovers, see construction debris removal and describe the materials.

Usable items are separated for donation or recycling when practical; not every piece can be reused. Send your Westland address, item list, photos, and preferred date through the fast estimate form, or call or text 734-480-8190. We will confirm the scope and appointment availability with you.
